A · Full Flight Choreography

The page is one continuous flight. Buttery smooth-scrolling (Lenis), a plane that traces a route line through the whole page as you scroll, pinned scenes that scrub with the wheel (hero takeoff, SkyMap orbit, chapter fly-through), altimeter-style progress HUD. Maximum "never seen before" factor — and the most build effort.
